▾📉 Back Test rolling replay model · what these wallets would have returned
A $1,000 paper account replaying a rolling window — "what if I'd started following these wallets 30 days ago" — over the wallet set in live/backtest.json (interchangeable: any wallet can be added or removed; currently the copy bot's follow set). Volume-class wallets mirror high-conviction bets (top 20% by stake, threshold from pre-window history); whale-class wallets mirror every bet. Entries above 95¢ are skipped (slippage + fees eat the payout). Stakes are dynamic: 4% of current equity per bet (Kelly-style compounding — bets grow with wins, shrink in drawdowns, halve below 80% of the equity peak), pinned at $250: once the book outgrows that stake level, surplus profit is swept to a banked reserve that never bets — a profit ratchet that also keeps fills inside realistic order-book depth. Each entry pays the real Polymarket taker fee (0.03 sports rate) and a lag-slippage haircut (~90s, +0.5% entry). Held to resolution; cash recycles as bets settle.

▾🎯 Sharp Wallets validated copyable · refreshed daily

Sharp wallets — validated copyable

The repeatable find: wallets whose large bets win on genuinely-uncertain markets, validated forward and then filtered to the ones that are actually profitable to copy — copy-positive with a real hold-to-resolution edge (win a clear majority of held conviction bets), not scalpers or last-minute snipers. The list and all stats (win %, record, P&L, lead) come from the winning-wallet-finder pipeline, refreshed daily. Copy P&L is the key number — what a flat-$50 copy of their conviction bets would have actually made since June 1 (mirroring their exits, settling held bets at true resolution). It cuts through high win rates that don't survive copying: a scalper can show ~100% conviction win % yet a negative Copy P&L. Conviction win %/record/P&L cover all of each wallet's top-20%-stake bets (a position stat); realized P&L is over their last 500 resolved bets. Click a name for their Polymarket profile.
