▾📉 Back Test rolling replay model · what these wallets would have returned
A $1,000 paper account replaying a rolling window — "what if I'd started following these wallets 30 days ago" — over the wallet set in live/backtest.json (interchangeable: any wallet can be added or removed; currently the copy bot's follow set). Volume-class wallets mirror high-conviction bets (top 20% by stake, threshold from pre-window history); whale-class wallets mirror every bet. Entries above 95¢ are skipped (slippage + fees eat the payout). Stakes are dynamic: the class % of current equity per bet (Kelly-style compounding — bets grow with wins, shrink in drawdowns, halve below 80% of the equity peak), and never larger than the followed wallet's own stake: when the percentage works out to more than they actually bet, the replay bets exactly their amount — you can't out-conviction the signal, and every fill stays within size the market demonstrably absorbed. Each entry pays the real Polymarket taker fee (0.03 sports rate) and a lag-slippage haircut (~90s, +0.5% entry). Exits mirror the signal, like the live bot: when the wallet fully closed a position before resolution the replay sells there too (exit fee + slippage paid, status SOLD); bets they held settle at the on-chain payout — 1/0, or 0.5 for 50/50 refunds (walkovers/abandonments, counted as scratches, not losses). Cash recycles as bets settle or exit.
