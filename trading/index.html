Sharp wallets — validated copyable

The repeatable find: wallets whose large bets win on genuinely-uncertain markets, validated forward and then filtered to the ones that are actually profitable to copy — copy-positive with a real hold-to-resolution edge (win a clear majority of held conviction bets), not scalpers or last-minute snipers. The list and all stats (win %, record, P&L, lead) come from the winning-wallet-finder pipeline, refreshed daily. Copy P&L is the key number — what a flat-$50 copy of their conviction bets would have actually made since June 1 (mirroring their exits, settling held bets at true resolution). It cuts through high win rates that don't survive copying: a scalper can show ~100% conviction win % yet a negative Copy P&L. Conviction win %/record/P&L cover all of each wallet's top-20%-stake bets (a position stat); realized P&L is over their last 500 resolved bets. Click a name for their Polymarket profile.
