Paper money, measurement mode. Study D trades T9's finding that same-outcome sibling markets reprice slowly: when an event group's most-printed market moves ≥10¢ inside 120s, the harness paper-FAKs $100 at each lagging sibling's live book (≤2 siblings, cooldown 10m/event) — but only within 4¢ of the sibling's stale print, and down-moves buy the sibling's complement token so a crashing book can't pose as a bargain. Every attempt logs the standing ask's premium over the stale print: median ≥ +8¢ over 3 days pre-registers the whole edge as a mirage (the tape "entered" at prints nobody still quotes) — a kill that needs no paper sample. Settles are provisional (CLOB flags) until the nightly re-grade against on-chain payout vectors (CHAIN badge); EV reports per episode and per dollar staked so partial fills can't flatter it. PASS ≥ +$4/$100 @ n≥400 & ≥5 days · KILL ≤ 0 @ n≥300 — the verdict binds to the chain-graded ledger per #23. Feed: wwf-lagbot.fly.dev/feed · refreshes every 20s.

Study C passed its tape-scored window (+$2.17/lean, n=20,461) with every entry booked at the lean-side last print — the same assumption that made Study B look profitable until a real book priced it at −$8.51/fill. This harness answers whether the lean side is actually buyable: when a screened maker-sharp's net inventory in one token crosses $150 at ≥60% one-sidedness (follow arm, $150–500), it paper-FAKs $100 at the live ask — but only within 3¢ of the print, and a net-short lean buys the complement token. The screened set is published nightly from strictly-prior tape, so the box can never select the wallets that happened to win during its own window. Median ask premium ≥ +8¢ over 3 days kills the study without needing a paper sample. PASS ≥ +$2/fill & hit ≥ .56 @ n≥400 & ≥5 days, all guards clear.
